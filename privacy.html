Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DNS Postmaster Tool ("Application") accesses, uses and protects information when users authorize the Application through Google OAuth.

1. Information We Access

With user authorization, the Application accesses Google Postmaster Tools API data, which may include domain reputation, spam-rate information and other available email deliverability metrics.

The Application may also process authentication information required to authorize API access.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the Application's email reputation monitoring, reporting and analytics functionality.

3. Google API Services

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Information Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Access to such data is limited to the purposes disclosed in this policy and the functionality authorized by the user. Any additional sharing or processing must be disclosed accurately and comply with applicable requirements.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information handled by the Application. Authentication credentials and access tokens should be protected against unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the Application's disclosed purposes and applicable requirements. Users may request deletion of their associated application data through the contact address provided below.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke the Application's access through their Google Account third-party connections and permissions settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
